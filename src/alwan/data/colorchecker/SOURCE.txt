ColorChecker references as colour-science carries them, after X-Rite's and
BabelColor's own tables: Classic pre-2014 and post-2014 D50, the 1976 C
rendering, BabelColor average xyY and reflectance, the Ohta reflectances, PMC,
Digital SG, and the Image Engineering TE226 v2 D65 set.
Written by gendata/data/reference_data.py and colorchecker_names.py.

classic_post2014_srgb_linear.csv: the Classic after November 2014 in linear sRGB (CAT02 from
D50), colour-checker-detection's orientation reference, written by
gendata/data/checker_detect_reference.py.

template_classic_size.csv, template_classic_centroids.csv, template_classic_colours.csv,
template_classic_correspondences.csv: the ColorChecker Classic template of
colour-checker-detection 0.2.3 (detection/templates/template_colorchecker_classic.npz,
Copyright 2018 Colour Developers, BSD-3-Clause), copied verbatim by
gendata/data/checker_templates.py for the templated checker detector.
