ColorChecker references as colour-science carries them, after X-Rite's and
BabelColor's own tables: Classic pre-2014 and post-2014 D50, the 1976 C
rendering, BabelColor average xyY and reflectance, the Ohta reflectances, PMC,
Digital SG, and the Image Engineering TE226 v2 D65 set.
Written by gendata/data/reference_data.py and colorchecker_names.py.
