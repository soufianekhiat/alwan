stain_matrices.csv: the eleven stain vector sets of scikit-image's
skimage/color/colorconv.py (rgb_from_hed, _hdx, _fgx, _bex, _rbd, _gdx, _hax,
_bro, _bpx, _ahx, _hpx) and the inverses it computes, BSD-3-Clause, the
scikit-image team. The H&E-DAB set is Ruifrok and Johnston (2001), "Quantification
of histochemical staining by color deconvolution", Anal. Quant. Cytol. Histol.
23(4); the others follow G. Landini's ImageJ colour deconvolution plugin, as
scikit-image cites. Read from the installed module, not retyped, by
alwan_dev/gendata/data/stain_matrices.py.
