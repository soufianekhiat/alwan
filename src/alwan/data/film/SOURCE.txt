Each <STOCK>.csv is the profiled stock as FilmSpectral computes it from the
digitised datasheet, on 380-780 nm at 10 nm, packed as gendata/data/film_stocks.py
documents. The four shared tables are the oracle's status densitometry and
printer light constants on the same grid. Written by gendata/data/film_stocks.py;
stocks.json lists what each profile is.
