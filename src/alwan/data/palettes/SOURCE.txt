Two palettes, two different positions on licensing.

freetone.inc
  Stuart Semple's Freetone, 1,310 colours in device CMYK, from
  FREETONE.ase in the published Adobe swatch bundle.
  LICENCE: NONE STATED. The bundle carries no licence text of any kind. It is
  vendored on the owner's decision rather than under a grant, and that is
  recorded here rather than left to be discovered.

css_color_3.inc
  The CSS Color Module Level 3 keywords, 147 colours, via colour-science's
  CSS_COLOR_3 after the W3C recommendation.
  colour-science 0.4.7, BSD-3-Clause (../LICENSE-colour-science.txt).
  The keyword list itself is W3C editorial content, not a dataset.

Both written by gendata/data/palettes.py.
