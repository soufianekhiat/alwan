Relative spectral power distributions from colour.SDS_ILLUMINANTS and
colour.SDS_LIGHT_SOURCES, on 360-830 nm at 1 nm, one file per illuminant: the CIE
A/B/C, D series, E, the FL and FL3.x fluorescents, the HP high-pressure lamps, the
LED series, and the measured light-source pack; iso7589/ holds the ISO 7589
photographic sources on 350-690 nm at 10 nm, the printer one on 350-560.
Written by gendata/data/illuminants_extended.py, illuminants_fl3_id.py,
light_sources.py and iso7589.py.
