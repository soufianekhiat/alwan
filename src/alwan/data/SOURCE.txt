The 34 CSV files beside this one, which are not in a subdirectory because they
are single tables rather than datasets.

Almost all of them are PUBLISHED FORMULA CONSTANTS, matrices and exponents read
out of the paper or standard that defines them, so they carry no licence of
their own. Each is written by a script in alwan_dev/gendata/data/, and that
script names the source it took the numbers from:

  ictcp_*        ITU-R BT.2100 ICtCp, the LMS and PQ/HLG matrices
  ipt_*          Ebner and Fairchild (1998) IPT
  igpgtg_*       Hellwig and Fairchild IgPgTg scale
  ihls_*         Hanbury (2003) improved HLS
  prolab_*       Konovalenko et al. (2021) ProLab projective matrices
  xyb_*          XYB (JPEG XL): the opsin matrix and bias as ColorAide (MIT)
                 carries them; the inverse and the XYZ matrices computed from it
  planckian_*    Krystek (1985) Planckian locus rational polynomial, via
                 colour.temperature.CCT_to_uv_Krystek1985
  ssi_*          Spectral Similarity Index weights, via
                 colour.quality.ssi.spectral_similarity_index
  srgb_*, rgb_*  sRGB and RGB working-space matrices
  white_*, hdr_* reference white points
  aces2_chroma_norm_{cos,sin}
                 ACES 2.0 chroma compression normalisation, four Fourier terms
                 each, approximating the AP1 gamut cusp M against hue angle.
                 From the Academy's ACES 2.0 Output Transform (aces-aswf/
                 aces-core), CTL lib/Lib.Academy.OutputTransform.ctl,
                 chroma_compress_norm(), cross-checked against OpenColorIO's
                 src/OpenColorIO/ops/fixedfunction/ACES2/Transform.cpp.
                 Written by gendata/data/aces2_fourier.py. The gamut cusp tables
                 those feed are in aces2/, which has its own SOURCE.txt.

THE THREE agx_* FILES ARE DIFFERENT, AND THEIR LICENCE IS NOT RECORDED ANYWHERE
IN THIS TREE.

  agx_blender_lut3d.csv          14 MB, Blender's AgX (EaryChow) baked from
                                 AgX_Base_sRGB.cube, a 57^3 3D LUT that carries
                                 gamut compression not decomposable into a matrix
                                 and a curve, which is why it ships as a cube
  agx_default_contrast_lut.csv   Troy Sobotka's AgX, LUTs/AgX_Default_Contrast.spi1d
  agx_sb2383_contrast_lut.csv    Troy Sobotka's SB2383 experiment

Each is baked from files vendored at alwan_dev/extern/agx/{blender,sobotka,
sb2383}, twelve tracked files in all, and NONE of those upstreams has a licence
file vendored beside it. The generators (gendata/data/agx_blender.py,
agx_contrast_lut.py, agx_sb2383.py) name where each came from and say nothing
about terms.

That is a gap, not a finding: it means nobody has written the terms down here,
not that the terms are bad. The project's own rule, section 4.1 of the release
plan, is to vendor only under a licence it has recorded, so these three want an
answer before the tag. Recorded here rather than left for someone to discover
from a 14 MB CSV with no provenance.
