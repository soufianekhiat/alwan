The colour matching functions of colour.MSDS_CMFS, resampled onto alwan's grid.
CIE 1931 2 deg, CIE 1964 10 deg, CIE 2015 2 and 10 deg, Stockman and Sharpe 2 and
10 deg, Smith and Pokorny 1975, Stiles and Burch 1955 2 deg and 1959 10 deg, and
Wright and Guild 1931, one file per channel on 360-830 nm at 1 nm. Zeros outside
a set's native range rather than an extrapolation, which is why several files
start and end in runs of 0.
Written by gendata/data/cmf.py.
